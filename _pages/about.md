<div class="lead">
  <p>I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Business Administration at the Business School at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. I hold a Master’s degree in Management Science and Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China. My research integrates data envelopment analysis (DEA), mathematical optimization, and machine learning for performance evaluation, benchmarking, and decision support. I study performance-oriented clustering and recommendation systems for digital platforms, alongside empirical modeling for sustainable operations, supply chains, and urban energy systems.</p>
  <p>My published work with Joe Zhu in <em>Annals of Operations Research</em> (2026) integrates DEA-based performance scores and benchmarking relationships with deep learning to support NFT recommendations. The recommendation system is evaluated using CryptoPunks data and comparisons with alternative recommendation methods. My other published work examines urban energy efficiency, digital economies, and smart manufacturing in <em>Journal of Cleaner Production</em>, <em>Computers &amp; Industrial Engineering</em>, and <em>Environment, Development and Sustainability</em>.</p>
  <p>My ongoing working paper, <em>NBDE: Nash Bargaining DEA-Enhanced Clustering Model</em>, with Joe Zhu develops performance-oriented clustering using Nash-bargaining DEA weight profiles as clustering criteria. It uses Evolutionary Monte Carlo to explore cluster partitions and weighted K-nearest neighbors (KNN) for out-of-sample assignment. Evaluation combines synthetic experiments, comparisons with alternative methods, and an application to Twitch streamers.</p>
</div>
